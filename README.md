# Cape AI Assistant

Build Cape Cleaning Co – AI Cleaning Operations Assistant

Create a modern, professional, responsive web application called Cape Cleaning Co – AI Cleaning Operations Assistant.

Cape Cleaning Co is a professional cleaning services company. The purpose of this application is to demonstrate how AI can improve productivity in a real-world service business by reducing repetitive administrative work, improving customer communication, organising cleaning jobs, and helping staff manage their daily tasks.

The application should be suitable for a CAPACITI AI workplace productivity project and should clearly demonstrate:

A real-world business use case

Effective AI-powered productivity features

Strong prompt engineering

Useful and accurate AI outputs

Responsible AI practices

A clear improvement in workplace productivity

The application should feel like a real business product rather than a basic prototype.

10. RESPONSIBLE AI PAGE

Create a page called:

Responsible AI

This page should explain how Cape Cleaning Co uses AI responsibly.

Include:

Human Review

AI-generated customer communications and important scheduling recommendations should be reviewed by a human employee.

Accuracy

The AI should not invent information. Users should verify important information before acting on it.

Privacy

Do not enter unnecessary sensitive customer information into AI prompts.

Transparency

Clearly indicate when content has been generated or assisted by AI.

Bias & Fairness

AI recommendations should not be used to unfairly discriminate against employees or customers.

AI Limitations

Explain that AI can make mistakes and should support—not replace—human judgement.

These responsible AI safeguards directly support the project requirement to identify limitations, risks, and validation steps.
